Ku batunze ibinyabiziga mu Rwanda, guhora umenya aho imodoka yawe iri ni ingenzi ku mutekano no ku mahoro yo mu mutima. Prime Track Africa itanga ibisubizo bishya bikwemerera gukurikirana imodoka yawe aho iri hose, kuva mu mihanda yuzuye abantu ya Kigali kugeza mu mihanda yo mu cyaro mu gihugu hose.

## Uko Prime Track Africa ikora

**Gukurikirana hakoreshejwe GPS ako kanya:** Ibikoresho byacu bigezweho bitanga amakuru y’ako kanya y’aho imodoka yawe iri.

**Uburyo bworoshye gukoresha:** Reba amakuru yo gukurikirana ukoresheje porogaramu ya telefoni cyangwa urubuga rwa interineti byakorewe koroha.

**Integuza ako kanya:** Habwa ubutumwa ku ngendo zidasanzwe cyangwa iyo imodoka yawe ivuye mu karere kagenwe.

## Inyungu ku batunze imodoka

**Umutekano wiyongereye:** Kumira ubujura kandi ugarure vuba imodoka yawe igihe bibaye ngombwa.

**Koroherwa:** Genzura uko imodoka yawe imeze uri kure kandi utegure umunsi wawe ushingiye ku makuru y’ako kanya.

**Igiciro gikwiye:** Ishimire gukurikirana kw’ireme ryo hejuru kutabangamiye ireme.

**Umwanzuro** Waba uparitse mu mujyi rwagati cyangwa ukora urugendo mu Rwanda hose, Prime Track Africa ituma uhora uhuye n’imodoka yawe. Menya ubwisanzure n’umutekano byo gukurikirana imodoka mu buryo bwizewe hamwe natwe.
