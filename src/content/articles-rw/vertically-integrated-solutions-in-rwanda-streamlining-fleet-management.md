Ibisubizo bihuriza byose ku mutanga serivisi umwe bitanga uburyo bworoshye kandi bunoze bwo gucunga ibinyabiziga mu Rwanda. Bihuza ibice byose by’igisubizo, uhereye ku bikoresho ukageza kuri porogaramu n’ubufasha, bigashyirwa ku mutanga serivisi umwe, bigatuma ubucuruzi bworoshya ibikorwa byabwo n’imikoranire n’abatanga serivisi.

Abatanga serivisi bahuriza byose hamwe bakora byose, kuva ku gushyiraho ibikoresho bya GPS kugeza ku gukora no kubungabunga porogaramu y’urubuga. Ibi bikuraho gukorana n’abatanga serivisi benshi, bikoroshya itumanaho kandi bikagabanya ibyago by’ibibazo byo guhuza sisitemu.

Imwe mu nyungu z’ingenzi z’ibisubizo bihuriza byose hamwe ni uko amakuru agenda neza kurushaho. Kubera ko ibice byose bya sisitemu bicungwa n’umutanga serivisi umwe, amakuru ahuzwa nta nkomyi kandi akaboneka byoroshye. Ibi biguha ishusho rusange y’imicungire y’ibinyabiziga byawe kandi bigatuma ufata ibyemezo bishingiye ku makuru kurushaho.

Byongeye kandi, abatanga serivisi bahuriza byose hamwe akenshi batanga ubufasha bwihariye kurushaho. Kubera ko bashinzwe ibice byose by’igisubizo, basobanukirwa neza ibyo ukeneye byihariye kandi bashobora kuguha ubufasha bujyanye nabyo kurushaho.

Mu bucuruzi bwo mu Rwanda burushaho kugorana, ibisubizo bihuriza byose hamwe bitanga uburyo bworoshye kandi bunoze bwo gucunga ibinyabiziga. Iyo ibice byose by’igisubizo bihurijwe ku mutanga serivisi umwe, ubucuruzi bushobora koroshya ibikorwa byabwo, kunoza uko amakuru agenda no kubona ubufasha bwihariye kurushaho.
