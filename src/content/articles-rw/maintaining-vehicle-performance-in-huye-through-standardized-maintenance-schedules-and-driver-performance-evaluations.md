Imiterere igoye y’ubutaka bwa Huye ivunisha cyane ibinyabiziga, bigasaba uburyo bwo kubibungabunga hakiri kare. Ingengabihe zisanzwe zo kubisana, zishingiye ku nama z’ababikora n’imiterere y’aho bikorera, ni ingenzi. Igenzura rihoraho, rikorwa n’abakanishi babifitiye ubushobozi, rituma ibibazo bishobora kuvuka bimenyekana kandi bigakemurwa vuba. Isuzuma ry’imikorere y’abashoferi rigira uruhare runini mu kwirinda kwangirika kw’ibinyabiziga kutari ngombwa. Amahugurwa yibanda ku buryo bwo gutwara buzigama lisansi no gufata neza ikinyabiziga ashobora kongera cyane igihe ibinyabiziga bimara. Kubika inyandiko zirambuye, zigaragaza ibikorwa byo gusana n’imikorere y’abashoferi, bitanga ubusobanuro bw’agaciro bwo kunoza ingengabihe zo gusana no kongera ubumenyi bw’abashoferi.
