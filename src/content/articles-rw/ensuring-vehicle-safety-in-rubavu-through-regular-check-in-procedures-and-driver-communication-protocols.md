Imiterere yihariye y’ubutaka bwa Rubavu n’ubukerarugendo bwaho bisaba ingamba zikomeye z’umutekano ku bikorwa by’ibinyabiziga. Gutanga raporo buri gihe, bisaba abashoferi kuvuga aho bari n’uko bimeze mu bihe byagenwe, bituma ibinyabiziga bikora mu mutekano. Amabwiriza yo kuvugana n’abashoferi, agaragaza uko bamenyesha ibihe byihutirwa n’uko bavugana n’abashinzwe kohereza imodoka, atuma impungenge zose z’umutekano zisubizwa vuba. Inama z’umutekano n’amahugurwa bikorwa buri gihe bishimangira imitwarire itekanye kandi bigatuma abashoferi biteguye guhangana n’ibihe ibyo ari byo byose. Guhuza gutanga raporo buri gihe n’amabwiriza asobanutse yo kuvugana n’abashoferi bifasha Rubavu kongera cyane umutekano w’ibinyabiziga.
