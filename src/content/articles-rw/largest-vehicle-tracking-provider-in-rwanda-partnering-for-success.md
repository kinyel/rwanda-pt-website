Guhitamo ikigo gikwiye cyo gukurikirana ibinyabiziga ni icyemezo gikomeye ku bucuruzi bwo mu Rwanda. Gufatanya n’ikigo kinini bifite ibyiza byinshi, birimo kwizerwa, kugera ahantu henshi n’ikoranabuhanga rigezweho. Ibi bigo bifite ubushobozi n’ubunararibonye bwo gutanga ibisubizo bigezweho bihuye n’ibikenewe bitandukanye by’ubucuruzi.

Ibigo binini bisanzwe bifite imiyoboro n’ibikorwa remezo byagutse, bigatuma ikurikiranwa ryizewe rigera mu Rwanda hose. Bishora cyane mu bushakashatsi n’iterambere, bihora binoza ikoranabuhanga ryabyo kandi bigatanga ibiranga n’imikorere bigezweho. Ibi bisobanura ko uzabona ibikoresho bigezweho kurusha ibindi byo gucunga ibinyabiziga byawe.

Byongeye kandi, ibigo binini bikunze gutanga serivisi nyinshi, zirimo gushyiraho ibikoresho, amahugurwa n’ubufasha buhoraho. Bifite amatsinda y’inzobere yihariye ashobora kugufasha gushyiraho sisitemu no kuyikoresha neza. Ibi bishobora kuba ingenzi cyane ku bucuruzi butangiye gukurikirana ibinyabiziga.

Gufatanya n’ikigo kinini binaguha amahoro yo mu mutima. Ushobora kwizera ko ukorana n’ikigo kizwi neza gifite amateka yagaragaye y’intsinzi. Gifite ubushobozi n’ubudahangarwa byo gushyigikira ibikenewe n’ubucuruzi bwawe ubu n’ahazaza.

Mu bukungu bw’u Rwanda bukura, guhitamo ikigo gikwiye cyo gukurikirana ibinyabiziga ni ingenzi ku ntsinzi y’igihe kirekire. Gufatanya n’ikigo kinini bikugeza ku ikoranabuhanga ryiza kurusha irindi, serivisi yizewe n’ubufasha bw’inzobere, bikagufasha kunoza imicungire y’ibinyabiziga byawe no kugera ku ntego z’ubucuruzi bwawe.
