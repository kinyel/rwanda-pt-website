Uko ibikenewe mu mutekano w’ibinyabiziga, imikorere myiza n’igenzura ry’ako kanya byiyongera, ibigo bikurikirana hakoreshejwe GPS mu Rwanda bigira uruhare rukomeye mu guhindura ubwikorezi n’imicungire y’ibinyabiziga. Ubucuruzi n’abantu ku giti cyabo bose bitabaza ibisubizo byo gukurikirana hakoreshejwe GPS kugira ngo barinde umutekano, bagabanye ibiciro kandi banoze imikorere y’ibinyabiziga muri rusange.

## Kuki wahitamo ikigo gikurikirana hakoreshejwe GPS mu Rwanda?

Ikigo cy’umwuga gikurikirana hakoreshejwe GPS gitanga ibisubizo bigezweho bijyanye n’imodoka z’abantu ku giti cyabo, ibinyabiziga by’ubucuruzi n’ibikorwa by’ubwikorezi. Ibi bisubizo bitanga gukurikirana ako kanya, imbibi zagenwe, kugenzura umuvuduko na telematiki igezweho kugira ngo binoze imikoreshereze n’umutekano by’ibinyabiziga.

## Serivisi z’ingenzi zitangwa n’ibigo bikurikirana hakoreshejwe GPS

### 1. Gukurikirana ibinyabiziga ako kanya

Sisitemu zo gukurikirana hakoreshejwe GPS zitanga amakuru y’aho ibinyabiziga biri ako kanya, bigafasha ubucuruzi n’abantu ku giti cyabo kugenzura ibinyabiziga byabo amasaha 24 kuri 24, iminsi 7 kuri 7. Ibi bitanga umutekano ukomeye n’ubugenzuzi bunoze ku bikorwa.

### 2. Ibisubizo byo gucunga ibinyabiziga

Ibigo bicunga ibinyabiziga byinshi bishobora kungukira ku micungire y’ibinyabiziga ishingiye kuri GPS. Ibi bisubizo bifasha gukurikirana imikorere y’ibinyabiziga, ikoreshwa rya lisansi, imyitwarire y’abashoferi n’ingengabihe yo kubisana.

### 3. Gukumira ubujura no kugarura ibinyabiziga

Iyo ibikoresho bya GPS bishyizwemo, ibinyabiziga byibwe bishobora kuboneka no kugarurwa vuba. Zimwe muri sisitemu zinemerera guhagarika ikinyabiziga uri kure kugira ngo hirindwe kugikoresha utabyemerewe.

### 4. Gukurikirana imyitwarire y’abashoferi

Ibisubizo byo gukurikirana hakoreshejwe GPS bikurikirana imitwarire nko kurenza umuvuduko, gufata feri bikabije no kwatsa moteri imodoka ihagaze. Ubucuruzi bushobora gukoresha aya makuru mu kongera umutekano w’abashoferi, kugabanya amafaranga ya lisansi no kunoza kubahiriza amategeko y’umuhanda.

### 5. Imbibi zagenwe no gutoranya inzira nziza

Imbibi zagenwe zemerera abakoresha gushyiraho imbibi, bakabona integuza iyo ikinyabiziga cyinjiye cyangwa kivuye ahantu hagenwe. Ibi bigirira akamaro cyane cyane ubucuruzi bukeneye kwemeza ko inzira zagenwe zubahirizwa.

## Akamaro ko gukorana n’ikigo gikurikirana hakoreshejwe GPS mu Rwanda

## Umutekano wiyongereye

Ibisubizo byo gukurikirana hakoreshejwe GPS bifasha gukumira ubujura bw’ibinyabiziga no kubigarura vuba iyo hari ikibaye.

## Imikorere myiza kurushaho

Abayobozi b’ibinyabiziga bashobora gutoranya inzira nziza, kugenzura uko ibinyabiziga bimeze no kugabanya ibiciro by’imikorere binyuze mu igenamigambi rinoze.

## Amafaranga ya lisansi agabanutse

Gukurikirana ikoreshwa rya lisansi n’imyitwarire y’abashoferi bifasha ubucuruzi kugabanya amafaranga ya lisansi atari ngombwa.

## Kubahiriza amategeko

Sisitemu zo gukurikirana hakoreshejwe GPS zifasha ibigo kubahiriza amategeko y’ubwikorezi y’u Rwanda, zemeza ko abashoferi bubahiriza imbibi z’umuvuduko zemewe n’amategeko n’inzira zagenwe.

## Guhitamo ikigo gikwiye gikurikirana hakoreshejwe GPS mu Rwanda

Mu guhitamo utanga serivisi zo gukurikirana hakoreshejwe GPS, tekereza kuri ibi bikurikira:

**Kwizerwa**: Hitamo ikigo gifite amateka meza n’ibitekerezo byiza by’abakiriya.

**Ikoranabuhanga n’ibiranga**: Shaka gukurikirana ako kanya, guhuzwa na porogaramu za telefoni n’isesengura rigezweho.

**Igiciro gikwiye**: Menya ko ibiciro bihuye n’ingengo y’imari yawe bitabangamiye ireme.

**Ubufasha ku bakiriya**: Ikigo cyizewe gikwiye gutanga serivisi nziza ku bakiriya n’ubufasha bwa tekiniki.

## Umwanzuro

Ikigo gikurikirana hakoreshejwe GPS mu Rwanda gishobora kongera cyane umutekano w’ibinyabiziga, kunoza imikorere y’ibinyabiziga no kugabanya ibiciro. Waba uri umuntu ku giti cye ushaka kurinda imodoka yawe cyangwa ubucuruzi bushaka imicungire myiza y’ubwikorezi, gushora mu gisubizo cyizewe cyo gukurikirana hakoreshejwe GPS ni icyemezo cy’ubwenge.
