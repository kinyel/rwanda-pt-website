Mu bucuruzi bwo mu Rwanda buhora butera imbere, gucunga ibinyabiziga neza ni ingenzi mu gutsinda. Ibisubizo byo gukurikirana ibinyabiziga bitanga ibikoresho bikenewe kugira ngo unoze ubwikorezi, wongere umutekano kandi unoze imikorere muri rusange. Ibi bisubizo biguha kubona ibinyabiziga byawe ako kanya, bikagufasha gufata ibyemezo bishingiye ku makuru bigira ingaruka kuri buri gice cy’ibikorwa byawe.

Uretse kumenya aho ibinyabiziga byawe biri gusa, ibisubizo byo gukurikirana ibinyabiziga bitanga imikorere itandukanye yakorewe koroshya imirimo. Urugero, gutoranya inzira nziza bisesengura imiterere y’urujya n’uruza n’uko imihanda imeze kugira ngo bimenye inzira zinoze kurushaho, bikagabanya ibilometero n’ikoreshwa rya lisansi. Imbibi zagenwe zigufasha gushyiraho imbibi, ukabona integuza iyo ibinyabiziga byinjiye cyangwa bivuye ahantu hagenwe, bikongera umutekano kandi bigatuma ibinyabiziga bikoreshwa icyo byagenewe.

Gukurikirana imyitwarire y’abashoferi ni ikindi kiranga cy’ingenzi. Gukurikirana ibipimo nko kurenza umuvuduko, gufata feri bikabije no kwatsa moteri igihe kirekire imodoka ihagaze bigufasha kumenya aho bikwiye kunozwa no gutanga amahugurwa agenewe buri wese, bigateza imbere imitwarire itekanye. Ibi ntibigabanya gusa ibyago by’impanuka, ahubwo binagira uruhare mu kuzigama lisansi no kugabanya kwangirika kw’ibinyabiziga.

Byongeye kandi, ibisubizo byo gukurikirana ibinyabiziga bitanga raporo n’isesengura byuzuye. Izi raporo zitanga ubusobanuro bw’agaciro ku bipimo by’ingenzi by’imikorere, bikagufasha kumenya ibitinza ibikorwa, kunoza uko umutungo ukoreshwa no gufata ibyemezo bishingiye ku makuru byongera imikorere myiza y’ibinyabiziga muri rusange. Mu isoko ry’u Rwanda rihatanirwa, ubu busobanuro bushingiye ku makuru ni ingenzi kugira ngo ukomeze kuba imbere.
