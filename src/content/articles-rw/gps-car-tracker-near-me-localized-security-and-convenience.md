Iyo ushakisha “igikoresho cya GPS cy’imodoka hafi yanjye,” uba ushaka igisubizo gihuza kuba hafi, kwizerwa no koroherwa. Abatanga serivisi bo mu Rwanda batanga serivisi n’ubufasha bijyanye n’ibyo ukeneye, kugira ngo ubone igikoresho gikubereye.

Guhitamo utanga serivisi wo hafi bifite ibyiza byinshi. Wungukira kuri serivisi yihariye, inama z’inzobere n’ubufasha bwa tekiniki bwihuse. Abashyiraho ibikoresho bo hafi bashobora kwemeza ko igikoresho gishyirwa mu modoka yawe neza nta nkomyi, bikagabanya igihe imodoka imara idakora kandi bikongera imikorere myiza. Byongeye kandi, abatanga serivisi bo hafi bazi neza imbogamizi n’ibikenewe byihariye by’akarere, bagatanga ibisubizo binogejwe bijyanye n’imiterere y’u Rwanda.

“Igikoresho cya GPS cy’imodoka hafi yanjye” bisobanura kandi koroherwa. Abatanga serivisi bo hafi bakunze kugira ingengabihe yoroshye yo gushyiraho ibikoresho n’ubufasha bubasanga aho bari, bigatuma gushyirwaho igikoresho no gutangira kugikoresha byoroha. Byongeye kandi, bashobora kugufasha kubungabunga igikoresho no gukemura ibibazo buri gihe, kugira ngo gikomeze gukora neza uko bishoboka.
