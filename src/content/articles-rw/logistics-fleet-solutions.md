Ubwikorezi bunoze ni inkingi y’ibikorwa byose by’ubwikorezi bitsinda. Prime Track Africa itanga ibisubizo by’ibinyabiziga by’ubwikorezi byakorewe koroshya ibikorwa, kugabanya igihe ibinyabiziga bimara bidakora no kongera umusaruro muri rusange mu Rwanda.

## Ibice by’ingenzi by’ibisubizo byacu by’ibinyabiziga by’ubwikorezi

**Sisitemu zo gukurikirana zihujwe:** Genzura ibinyabiziga byawe byose ako kanya kugira ngo ubone ishusho nziza.

**Kohereza imodoka mu buryo bwikora:** Menya neza ko ibicuruzwa bigezwa ku gihe kandi inzira zicungwa neza.

**Gutegura igihe cyo gusana:** Cunga gusana ibinyabiziga hakiri kare kugira ngo wirinde gupfa mu buryo butunguranye.

## Inyungu ku bucuruzi

**Imikorere myiza:** Gabanya ubukererwe kandi wongere kwizerwa k’urusobe rwawe rw’ubwikorezi.

**Kugabanya ibiciro:** Gabanya ikoreshwa rya lisansi n’ibiciro byo gusana ukoresheje ikurikiranwa n’isesengura binoze.

**Serivisi nziza ku bakiriya:** Geza ibicuruzwa ku gihe ukoresheje ibikoresho binoze byo gukurikirana no gucunga.

Ku bigo by’ubwikorezi bishaka kunoza imicungire y’ibinyabiziga byabyo mu Rwanda, Prime Track Africa itanga ibisubizo bijyanye n’ibyo bikeneye byongera imikorere myiza no kunoza itangwa rya serivisi. Yakira uburyo bw’ubwenge kurushaho bw’ubwikorezi hamwe na sisitemu yacu ihuza byose.
