Sisitemu yuzuye yo gucunga ibinyabiziga ni ingenzi ku bucuruzi bufite ibinyabiziga mu Rwanda. Izi sisitemu zitanga urubuga rumwe rwo gucunga ibice byose by’ibinyabiziga byawe, kuva ku gukurikirana aho biri kugeza ku gusesengura imikorere y’abashoferi. Sisitemu ishyizweho neza ishobora kongera cyane imikorere myiza, kugabanya ibiciro no kunoza imikorere muri rusange.

Sisitemu yo gucunga ibinyabiziga mu Rwanda ihuza imikorere itandukanye, irimo gukurikirana hakoreshejwe GPS, gutoranya inzira nziza, gukurikirana imyitwarire y’abashoferi no gukurikirana igihe cyo gusana ibinyabiziga. Ubu buryo buhuza byose buguha ishusho rusange y’imicungire y’ibinyabiziga byawe, bikagufasha gufata ibyemezo bishingiye ku makuru bigira ingaruka kuri buri gice cy’ubwikorezi bwawe.

Imwe mu nyungu z’ingenzi za sisitemu yo gucunga ibinyabiziga ni itumanaho rinoze. Sisitemu itanga amakuru y’ako kanya ku hantu ibinyabiziga biri, uko abashoferi bameze n’ingengabihe yo kugeza ibicuruzwa, bigatuma abashinzwe kohereza imodoka, abashoferi n’abakiriya bavugana nta nkomyi. Iri tumanaho rinoze rituma habaho ihuzabikorwa ryiza n’imikorere inoze kurushaho. Byongeye kandi, sisitemu yo gucunga ibinyabiziga ishobora gukora imirimo myinshi mu buryo bwikora, nko gukora raporo no gutegura igihe cyo gusana. Ibi biha abayobozi b’ibinyabiziga igihe cy’agaciro, bakibanda ku bikorwa by’ingenzi by’igihe kirekire. Sisitemu inatanga amakuru n’isesengura by’agaciro, bigufasha kumenya aho ukwiye kunoza no kunoza imicungire y’ibinyabiziga byawe.
