Kurinda imodoka yawe mu Rwanda bisaba igikoresho cyo kuyikurikirana cyizewe kandi gikora neza. Igikoresho “cyiza” kirenga gutanga aho iri gusa; gitanga uruhererekane rw’ibiranga byakorewe kurinda imodoka yawe no kuguha amahoro yo mu mutima. Ibi bikoresho byubakiwe gukora neza kandi kwizerwa, bigatuma ikurikiranwa rikomeza ndetse no ahantu hagoye.

Ibikoresho byiza byo gukurikirana imodoka mu Rwanda bishyira imbere ubusobanuro no gusubiza vuba. Bitanga amakuru nyayo y’aho imodoka iri nta gutinda, bikagufasha gukurikirana ingendo zayo ako kanya. Ibindi biranga bigezweho nko guhagarika moteri, kuyizimya uri kure no guhuzwa n’induru y’umutekano byongera umutekano, bikaguha ubugenzuzi ku modoka yawe ndetse n’igihe utari kumwe nayo.

Kuramba no kumara igihe kirekire na byo ni ingenzi. Ibikoresho byiza byo gukurikirana imodoka byubakiwe guhangana n’ikoreshwa rya buri munsi n’ihindagurika ry’ikirere. Akenshi bifite bateri zimara igihe kirekire n’imiterere idashobora kwangizwa byoroshye, bigatuma bikora ubudahagarara. Byongeye kandi, uburyo bworoshye gukoresha na porogaramu za telefoni bituma kugera ku makuru yo gukurikirana no kuyasobanukirwa byoroha, ubugenzuzi bukaba mu biganza byawe.
