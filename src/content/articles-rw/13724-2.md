## Serivisi nziza yo gukurikirana ibinyabiziga mu Rwanda: kunoza ubwikorezi bw’ubucuruzi

Ku bucuruzi bwo mu Rwanda bucunga ibinyabiziga byinshi, serivisi “nziza” yo kubikurikirana ni ingenzi mu kunoza ubwikorezi no kongera umusaruro. Izi serivisi zitanga uruhererekane rw’ibikoresho byakorewe kunoza imicungire y’ibinyabiziga no kugabanya ibiciro by’imikorere.

Serivisi nziza zo gukurikirana ibinyabiziga mu Rwanda zishyira imbere amakuru y’ako kanya n’ubusobanuro bushobora kugirwaho igikorwa. Zitanga raporo zirambuye ku hantu ibinyabiziga biri, imyitwarire y’abashoferi, ikoreshwa rya lisansi n’ingengabihe yo kubisana. Ayo makuru afasha abayobozi b’ibinyabiziga gufata ibyemezo bishingiye ku makuru ku bijyanye no gutoranya inzira nziza, kohereza imodoka no kugabanya umutungo. Ibindi biranga bigezweho nk’imbibi zagenwe, integuza n’isesengura ry’amakuru yabanje biguha ubugenzuzi bwuzuye ku bikorwa by’ibinyabiziga.

Kwaguka no guhuzwa n’izindi sisitemu na byo ni ingenzi. Serivisi nziza zo gukurikirana ibinyabiziga zakorewe gukura hamwe n’ubucuruzi bwawe no guhuzwa nta nkomyi n’izindi sisitemu usanzwe ukoresha. Zifite uburyo bworoshye gukoresha na porogaramu za telefoni, bigatuma kugera ku makuru y’ibinyabiziga no kuyacunga byoroha aho uri hose. Byongeye kandi, zitanga ubufasha bwizewe ku bakiriya n’amahugurwa, kugira ngo ubyaze umusaruro ushoboka ishoramari ryawe.
