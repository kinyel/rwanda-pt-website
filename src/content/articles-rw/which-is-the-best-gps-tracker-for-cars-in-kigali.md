I Kigali, ibikenewe mu sisitemu zizewe zo gukurikirana hakoreshejwe GPS biri ku rwego rwo hejuru kurusha mbere hose. Prime Track Africa yiyemeje gutanga igikoresho cya GPS cyiza gihuza ikoranabuhanga rigezweho no koroha kugikoresha.

## Ibiranga by’ingenzi by’igikoresho cyacu cya GPS

**Ubusobanuro n’ukuri:** Igikoresho cyacu gitanga amakuru nyayo y’aho imodoka iri ako kanya, ushobora kwizera.

**Ubushobozi bwo gushyiraho imbibi:** Shyiraho imbibi maze uhabwe integuza iyo imodoka yawe yinjiye cyangwa ivuye ahantu hagenwe.

**Gukomera no kuramba:** Cyakorewe guhangana n’imbogamizi zo gutwara mu mujyi no mu cyaro i Kigali.

## Impamvu Prime Track Africa itandukanye n’abandi

**Guhuzwa nta nkomyi:** Genzura byoroshye uko imodoka yawe imeze ukoresheje porogaramu yacu ya telefoni yoroshye kumva.

**Integuza zihindurwa uko ubishaka:** Hindura ubutumwa bukugeraho bujyane n’ibyo ukeneye byihariye, kugira ngo ubone amakuru ku gihe kandi ufate ingamba hakiri kare.

**Serivisi yibanda ku mukiriya:** Itsinda ryacu ry’ubufasha ryihariye rihari kugira ngo rigufashe kuri buri ntambwe.

Ku bashoferi b’i Kigali bashaka gukurikirana ibinyabiziga mu buryo bwiza kurusha ubundi, Prime Track Africa itanga kwizerwa n’imikorere bitagira uko bingana. Irebere impamvu igikoresho cyacu cya GPS gifatwa nk’amahitamo meza kurusha ayandi muri aka karere.
