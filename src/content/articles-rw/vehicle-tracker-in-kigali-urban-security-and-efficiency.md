Mu mujyi wa Kigali wuzuyemo ibikorwa, igikoresho cyo gukurikirana ibinyabiziga ni ingenzi ku mutekano no ku mikorere myiza. Ibikoresho byo gukurikirana ibinyabiziga i Kigali bitanga igenzura ry’aho biri ako kanya, imbibi zagenwe n’isesengura ry’amakuru yabanje, bigatanga ubusobanuro bwuzuye ku ngendo n’imikoreshereze y’ibinyabiziga.

Ku bucuruzi bukorera i Kigali, ibikoresho byo gukurikirana ibinyabiziga byoroshya ubwikorezi kandi binoza inzira zo kugeza ibicuruzwa. Iyo bakurikirana aho ibinyabiziga biri n’uko umuhanda umeze, abashinzwe kohereza imodoka bashobora gutanga imirimo neza no kugabanya ubukererwe. Ibyo bizana serivisi nziza ku bakiriya n’ibiciro by’imikorere bigabanutse. Gukurikirana ako kanya binafasha gucunga imyitwarire y’abashoferi, bikemeza ko amategeko y’umuhanda yubahirizwa kandi bigateza imbere imitwarire itekanye.

Ku muntu ku giti cye, igikoresho cyo gukurikirana ibinyabiziga i Kigali gitanga amahoro yo mu mutima kuko kirinda umutungo we. Integuza z’imbibi zagenwe zimenyesha abakoresha ingendo z’ikinyabiziga zitemewe, naho gukurikirana ako kanya bigafasha kugarura vuba ikinyabiziga cyibwe. Byongeye kandi, isesengura ry’amakuru yabanje ritanga ubusobanuro bw’agaciro ku buryo ikinyabiziga gikoreshwa, bigafasha abakoresha kumenya ibibazo bishobora kuvuka no kunoza ingengabihe yo kugisana.
