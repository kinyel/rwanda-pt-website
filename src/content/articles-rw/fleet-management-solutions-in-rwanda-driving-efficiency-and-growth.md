Mu Rwanda, aho ubujura bw’ibinyabiziga n’imikorere idanoze bishobora kugira ingaruka zikomeye ku bucuruzi, gushora mu bikoresho byiza byo gukurikirana imodoka ni icyemezo cy’ubwenge. Ibi bikoresho bitanga urwego rw’ingenzi rw’umutekano kandi bigatanga amakuru y’agaciro yo kunoza imicungire y’ibinyabiziga.

Ibikoresho bigezweho byo gukurikirana imodoka birenga kumenya aho iri hakoreshejwe GPS gusa. Bitanga ibiranga bitandukanye byongera umutekano, binoza imyitwarire y’abashoferi kandi bitanga ubusobanuro bw’agaciro ku mikorere y’ibinyabiziga. Gukurikirana ako kanya bigufasha kumenya neza aho ibinyabiziga byawe biri igihe icyo ari cyo cyose, bigakumira ubujura kandi bigafasha kugarura ikinyabiziga cyibwe. Ubushobozi bwo gushyiraho imbibi bwongera umutekano, kuko bukwemerera gushyiraho imbibi ukabona integuza iyo ibinyabiziga byinjiye cyangwa bivuye ahantu hagenwe.

Uretse umutekano, ibikoresho byo gukurikirana imodoka bigira uruhare runini mu kunoza imicungire y’ibinyabiziga. Amakuru ibi bikoresho bikusanya ashobora gukoreshwa mu kunoza itegurwa ry’inzira, gukurikirana imyitwarire y’abashoferi no kumenya aho imikoreshereze y’ibinyabiziga yanozwa. Bimwe muri ibi bikoresho bitanga n’ubusobanuro ku isuzuma ry’ibinyabiziga, bikagufasha gukemura hakiri kare ibibazo byo kubisana no kwirinda gupfa bihenze.

Mu guhitamo igikoresho cyo gukurikirana imodoka mu Rwanda, ni ngombwa kwita ku bintu byinshi, birimo ubusobanuro, kwizerwa n’igihe bateri imara. Shaka ibikoresho bitanga ikurikiranwa ry’ako kanya nta gutinda kandi bitanga amakuru nyayo y’aho imodoka iri ndetse no ahantu hagoye. Kwizerwa ni iby’ibanze, kuko ukeneye igikoresho gikora buri gihe nta guhagarara. Bateri imara igihe kirekire na yo ni ingenzi, cyane cyane ku binyabiziga bikora ingendo ndende cyangwa bikorera ahantu hitaruye.

Byongeye kandi, tekereza ku biranga igikoresho cyo gukurikirana imodoka gitanga. Ese ukeneye ubushobozi bwo gushyiraho imbibi? Ese isuzuma ry’ibinyabiziga ni ingenzi ku bucuruzi bwawe? Hitamo igikoresho gifite ibiranga bihuye neza n’ibyo ukeneye byihariye. Gushora mu bikoresho byiza byo gukurikirana imodoka ni intambwe ifatwa hakiri kare yo kurinda umutungo wawe no kunoza imicungire y’ibinyabiziga byawe mu Rwanda. Uhisemo igikoresho gikwiye, ugira amahoro yo mu mutima uzi ko ibinyabiziga byawe biri mu mutekano kandi ko ufite amakuru ukeneye kugira ngo wongere imikorere myiza kandi ugabanye ibiciro.
