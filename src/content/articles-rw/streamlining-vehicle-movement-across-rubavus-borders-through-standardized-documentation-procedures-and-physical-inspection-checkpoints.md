Kuba Rubavu iri ku mupaka bisaba ko ingendo z’ibinyabiziga zambuka imipaka zigenda neza kandi mu mutekano. Uburyo busanzwe bw’ibyangombwa, bugaragaza neza impapuro zisabwa n’amabwiriza ya gasutamo, bugabanya ubukererwe kandi bukemeza ko amabwiriza yubahirizwa. Amabariyeri y’igenzura, afite abakozi bahuguwe, akora igenzura ryimbitse ry’ibinyabiziga n’imizigo. Amahugurwa ahoraho ku bakozi bo ku mipaka atuma bamenya amabwiriza agezweho n’uburyo bwo kugenzura. Guhuza uburyo busanzwe bw’ibyangombwa n’amabariyeri y’igenzura bifasha Rubavu koroshya ingendo z’ibinyabiziga zambuka imipaka no kongera umutekano ku mipaka.
