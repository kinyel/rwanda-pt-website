description: "Ubwiyongere bw’urujya n’uruza i Muhanga busaba ingamba zinoze zo kurucunga. Gushyira ibyapa ahantu hatoranyijwe neza, bigaragaza neza inzira,"
date: 2025-03-03
author: "primeTEAM"
---

Ubwiyongere bw’urujya n’uruza i Muhanga busaba ingamba zinoze zo kurucunga. Gushyira ibyapa ahantu hatoranyijwe neza, bigaragaza neza inzira, imbibi z’umuvuduko n’aho guparika, biyobora abashoferi kandi bikagabanya urujijo. Abakozi bagenzura urujya n’uruza aho bibera, boherezwa mu masaha y’umubyigano no mu masangano y’imihanda y’ingenzi, bayobora urujya n’uruza kandi bakumira umubyigano. Ubushakashatsi bukorwa buri gihe ku rujya n’uruza n’isesengura ry’imiterere yarwo bifasha guhitamo aho ibyapa bishyirwa n’aho abakozi boherezwa. Guhuza ibyapa bishyizwe ahantu hatoranyijwe n’igenzura ry’urujya n’uruza aho bibera bifasha Muhanga gucunga neza urujya n’uruza rw’ibinyabiziga no kwemeza ko bigenda neza.
