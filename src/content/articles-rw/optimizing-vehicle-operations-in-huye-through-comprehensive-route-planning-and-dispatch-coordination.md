Imiterere yihariye y’ubutaka bwa Huye n’ibikorwa remezo bigenda byiyongera bisaba igenamigambi ryitondewe kugira ngo ibinyabiziga bikore neza. Aho kwishingikiriza ku gukurikirana ako kanya, sisitemu ikomeye y’inzira zateguwe mbere iba iy’ibanze. Ibi bisaba gushushanya neza inzira zikunze gukoreshwa, hitawe ku miterere y’urujya n’uruza, uko imihanda imeze n’ingengabihe yo kugeza ibicuruzwa. Ihuzabikorwa ryo kohereza imodoka riyobowe hamwe rigira uruhare runini. Ikigo cyagenewe kohereza imodoka, gifite abakozi b’inararibonye, kiba nk’ubwonko bw’ingendo z’ibinyabiziga. Kuvugana neza n’abashoferi no kubahiriza ingengabihe zagenwe mbere bituma ubukererwe bugabanuka kandi umutungo ugakoreshwa neza. Ubu buryo, nubwo busaba igenamigambi rikomeye, butuma ibinyabiziga bikora neza kandi mu buryo buteganyijwe muri Huye hose.
