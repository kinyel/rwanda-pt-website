Serivisi zo gukurikirana ibinyabiziga mu Rwanda ziha ubucuruzi ibisubizo bitandukanye byo kongera umutekano, kunoza imikorere no kugenzura neza kurushaho ibinyabiziga byabwo. Izi serivisi zikoresha ikoranabuhanga rya GPS mu gutanga amakuru y’aho biri ako kanya n’andi makuru y’agaciro.

Serivisi zo gukurikirana ibinyabiziga zishobora guhuzwa n’ibyo ubucuruzi butandukanye bukeneye byihariye. Waba ucunga ibinyabiziga bike byo kugeza ibicuruzwa cyangwa amakamyo menshi, hari ibisubizo biboneka byagufasha kunoza ibikorwa byawe. Izi serivisi akenshi zirimo ibiranga nko gukurikirana ako kanya, imbibi zagenwe, gukurikirana imyitwarire y’abashoferi na raporo.

Imwe mu nyungu z’ibanze za serivisi zo gukurikirana ibinyabiziga ni umutekano wiyongereye. Kumenya aho ibinyabiziga byawe biri igihe cyose bikumira ubujura kandi bigafasha kugarura ikinyabiziga cyibwe. Ubushobozi bwo gushyiraho imbibi bukwemerera gushyiraho imbibi ukabona integuza iyo ibinyabiziga byinjiye cyangwa bivuye ahantu hagenwe, bikarushaho kongera umutekano.

Serivisi zo gukurikirana ibinyabiziga zinagira uruhare mu kunoza imikorere. Amakuru akusanywa ashobora gukoreshwa mu gutoranya inzira nziza, gukurikirana imyitwarire y’abashoferi no kumenya aho imikoreshereze y’ibinyabiziga yanozwa. Ibyo bishobora gutuma amafaranga ya lisansi agabanuka, imikorere y’abashoferi ikiyongera n’imicungire y’ibinyabiziga muri rusange ikarushaho kuba myiza.
