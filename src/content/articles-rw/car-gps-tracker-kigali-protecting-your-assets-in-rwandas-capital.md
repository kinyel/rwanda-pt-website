I Kigali, umurwa mukuru w’u Rwanda wuzuyemo ibikorwa, ubujura bw’ibinyabiziga n’imikorere idanoze bishobora kugira ingaruka zikomeye ku bucuruzi. Igikoresho cya GPS cy’imodoka gitanga urwego rw’ingenzi rw’umutekano kandi kigatanga amakuru y’agaciro yo kunoza imikoreshereze y’ibinyabiziga. Ibi bikoresho bito ariko bifite imbaraga biguha amahoro yo mu mutima kandi bigufasha kurinda umutungo wawe w’agaciro.

Igikoresho cya GPS cy’imodoka i Kigali gitanga amakuru y’aho iri ako kanya, bikagufasha kumenya neza aho imodoka yawe iri igihe icyo ari cyo cyose. Ibi ni ingenzi ku mutekano, kuko bikumira ubujura kandi bigafasha kugarura imodoka iyo yibwe. Ubushobozi bwo gukurikirana aho imodoka yawe iri bunagufasha kugenzura uko ikoreshwa, ukamenya ko ikoreshwa icyo yagenewe.

Uretse umutekano, igikoresho cya GPS cy’imodoka gishobora no kongera imikorere myiza. Amakuru gikusanya ashobora gukoreshwa mu gutoranya inzira nziza, gukurikirana imyitwarire y’abashoferi no kumenya aho imikoreshereze y’ibinyabiziga yanozwa. Ibyo bishobora gutuma amafaranga ya lisansi agabanuka, imikorere y’abashoferi ikiyongera, n’imicungire y’ibinyabiziga muri rusange ikarushaho kuba myiza.

Mu guhitamo igikoresho cya GPS cy’imodoka i Kigali, tekereza ku bintu nk’ubusobanuro, kwizerwa n’igihe bateri imara. Igikoresho gikwiye gutanga amakuru nyayo y’aho imodoka iri kandi kigakora buri gihe nta guhagarara. Bateri imara igihe kirekire ni ingenzi, cyane cyane ku binyabiziga bikora ingendo ndende cyangwa bikorera ahantu hitaruye.
