Uko ubucuruzi bukura, ni ko n’imicungire y’ibinyabiziga irushaho kugorana. Urubuga rwa Prime Track Africa rwo gucunga ibinyabiziga rushobora kwaguka rwakorewe guhuza no kwaguka hamwe n’ibyo ubucuruzi bwawe bukeneye i Kigali no hirya yaho.

## Ibiranga by’ingenzi by’urubuga

**Kugera kuri byose hifashishijwe interineti:** Cunga ibinyabiziga byawe aho uri hose, ubona amakuru y’ako kanya abikwa kuri interineti.

**Imbonerahamwe zihindurwa uko ubishaka:** Hindura imbonerahamwe yawe kugira ngo yerekane ibipimo by’ingenzi kurusha ibindi ku bucuruzi bwawe.

**Isesengura riteganya:** Koresha ubusobanuro bushingiye ku makuru kugira ngo umenye mbere igihe cyo gusana kandi unoze imikorere y’ibinyabiziga.

## Impamvu ubushobozi bwo kwaguka ari ingenzi

**Guhuza n’iterambere:** Urubuga rwacu rukura hamwe n’ubucuruzi bwawe, rukakira ibinyabiziga n’inzira byiyongera.

**Kuzigama amafaranga:** Agura ibikorwa byawe udakeneye kuvugurura bihenze cyangwa izindi sisitemu z’inyongera.

**Ubugenzuzi bwiyongereye:** Ishimire kugenzura no kuyobora byuzuye ibikorwa by’ibinyabiziga byawe binyuze muri sisitemu imwe ihuza byose.

Urubuga rwa Prime Track Africa rwo gucunga ibinyabiziga rushobora kwaguka ni amahitamo y’ubwenge ku bucuruzi bwo i Kigali bushaka koroshya ibikorwa no gushyigikira iterambere. Koresha ikoranabuhanga ryacu kugira ngo ugumane imikorere myiza n’ubugenzuzi kuri buri cyiciro cy’iterambere ryawe.
