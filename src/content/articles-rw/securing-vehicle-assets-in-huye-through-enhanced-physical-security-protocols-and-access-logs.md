---

Iyo nta gukurikirana hakoreshejwe GPS, ingamba z’umutekano ufatika ziba inkingi yo kurinda ibinyabiziga i Huye. Ibi bisaba gushyiraho uburyo bufite ibyiciro byinshi, uhereye ku hantu ho guparika harinzwe hafite uburyo bukomeye bwo gufunga na kamera z’umutekano. Inyandiko z’abinjira n’abasohoka, zibikwa neza cyane, zandika buri kinyabiziga cyinjiye n’icyasohotse, zigatanga ibimenyetso bisobanutse byo kugenzura. Amarondo ahoraho akorwa n’abashinzwe umutekano agaragara nk’igikumira kandi atuma habaho gusubiza vuba igihe umutekano uhungabanye. Byongeye kandi, gusuzuma neza amateka y’abashoferi n’abakozi bishimangira ubunyangamugayo bwa sisitemu y’umutekano. Guhuza ingamba z’umutekano ufatika no kubika inyandiko neza bituma ibinyabiziga birindwa neza i Huye.
