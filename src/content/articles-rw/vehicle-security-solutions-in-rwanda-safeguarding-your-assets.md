Uko ibyaha bijyanye n’ibinyabiziga bikomeza kwiyongera, gushora mu bisubizo byizewe by’umutekano w’ibinyabiziga byabaye ngombwa kuri ba nyir’imodoka n’ubucuruzi mu Rwanda. Kuva ku bikoresho birwanya ubujura kugeza ku gukurikirana kugezweho hakoreshejwe GPS, sisitemu z’umutekano zigezweho zitanga uburinzi bwuzuye ku bujura, kwinjira utabyemerewe no gukoresha nabi.

## Ubwiyongere bw’ibikenewe mu bisubizo by’umutekano w’ibinyabiziga

Uko umubare w’abatunze ibinyabiziga mu Rwanda wiyongera, ibyago by’umutekano nk’ubujura bw’imodoka, kwangiza nkana no kuzikoresha utabyemerewe na byo byariyongereye. Uburyo bwa kera nk’ingufuri zisanzwe ntibukihagije, bigatuma ibisubizo by’umutekano by’ikoranabuhanga rigezweho biba ngombwa.

## Ikoranabuhanga rigezweho ry’umutekano w’ibinyabiziga

### 1. Gukurikirana hakoreshejwe GPS no kugenzura ako kanya

Sisitemu z’umutekano zigezweho zihuza gukurikirana hakoreshejwe GPS, zigatuma aho ikinyabiziga kiri kigenzurwa ako kanya. Ba nyir’ibinyabiziga bahabwa integuza ako kanya igihe habaye ingendo ziteye amakenga cyangwa kwinjira utabyemerewe.

### 2. Guhagarika ikinyabiziga uri kure

Kimwe mu biranga by’umutekano bikora neza cyane ni ukuhagarika ikinyabiziga uri kure. Ibi byemerera ba nyir’ibinyabiziga cyangwa abayobozi b’ibinyabiziga kuzimya moteri bari kure, bikabuza abajura gutoroka n’ikinyabiziga.

### 3. Induru n’ibikoresho birwanya ubujura

Sisitemu z’induru zigezweho zivuza induru zikomeye kandi zikohereza ubutumwa iyo hagaragaye kwinjira utabyemerewe. Izi nduru zikumira abagizi ba nabi bashobora kugerageza kwiba.

### 4. Kugenzura abinjira hakoreshejwe ibiranga umubiri

Zimwe mu modoka zigezweho ubu zifite ikoranabuhanga ryo kumenya igikumwe cyangwa isura kugira ngo abemerewe gusa ari bo bashobora kwatsa ikinyabiziga. Ibi byongera urundi rwego rw’umutekano.

### 5. Kamera zo mu modoka n’igenzura rya AI

Kamera zo mu modoka ntizitanga gusa ibimenyetso igihe habaye impanuka, ahubwo ziba n’ingamba y’umutekano. Sisitemu z’igenzura zikoresha AI zishobora kumenya ibikorwa bidasanzwe biri hafi y’ikinyabiziga zikabimenyesha nyiracyo.

## Akamaro k’ibisubizo by’umutekano w’ibinyabiziga

## Gukumira ubujura no gukoresha utabyemerewe

Gushyiraho ingamba z’umutekano nko gukurikirana hakoreshejwe GPS no guhagarika ikinyabiziga bifasha ba nyir’ibinyabiziga kugabanya cyane ibyago by’ubujura.

## Umutekano wiyongereye ku bashoferi n’abagenzi

Ibiranga by’umutekano bigezweho ntibirinda ikinyabiziga gusa, ahubwo binarinda umutekano w’abari muri cyo. Ibiranga byo gutabara mu bihe byihutirwa
