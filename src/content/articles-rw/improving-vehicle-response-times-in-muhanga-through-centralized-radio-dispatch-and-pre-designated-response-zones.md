I Muhanga, gusubiza vuba ni ingenzi kuri serivisi z’ubutabazi no kugeza ibicuruzwa byihutirwa. Sisitemu yo kohereza imodoka iyobowe hamwe kuri radiyo, ifite abayikoresha b’inararibonye, ituma itumanaho ryihuta n’ihuzabikorwa rinoze. Uturere tw’ubutabazi twagenwe mbere, dusobanuwe neza kandi tumenyeshejwe, dufasha abashinzwe kohereza imodoka kumenya vuba ikinyabiziga kiri hafi kandi kiboneka. Imyitozo n’igerageza bikorwa buri gihe bituma abakozi bamenya neza amabwiriza yo gutabara kandi bagashobora kwitwara neza mu bihe by’igitutu. Guhuza kohereza imodoka biyobowe hamwe kuri radiyo n’uturere twagenwe mbere bifasha Muhanga kongera cyane ubushobozi bwo gutabara mu bihe byihutirwa no gutanga serivisi zihutirwa ku gihe.
