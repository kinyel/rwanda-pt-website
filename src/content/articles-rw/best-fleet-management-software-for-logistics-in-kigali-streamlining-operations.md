Mu isoko ry’ubwikorezi rihatanirwa cyane i Kigali, porogaramu “nziza” yo gucunga ibinyabiziga ni ingenzi mu kunoza imikorere no kongera umusaruro. Izi porogaramu zitanga uruhererekane rw’ibikoresho byakorewe kunoza ubwikorezi no gucunga ibinyabiziga neza kurushaho.

Porogaramu nziza yo gucunga ibinyabiziga by’ubwikorezi i Kigali ishyira imbere amakuru y’ako kanya n’ibikorwa byikora. Itanga raporo zirambuye ku hantu ibinyabiziga biri, imikorere y’abashoferi n’ingengabihe yo kugeza ibicuruzwa. Ibindi biranga bigezweho nko gutoranya inzira nziza, kohereza imodoka n’integuza zikora ubwazo bifasha abayobozi b’ubwikorezi gufata ibyemezo bishingiye ku makuru no kugabanya ubukererwe. Guhuzwa n’izindi sisitemu z’ubwikorezi, nk’izo gucunga ububiko n’izo gucunga imikoranire n’abakiriya, bituma amakuru ahererekanwa nta nkomyi kandi imikorere ikagenda neza.

Koroha kuyikoresha no kwaguka na byo ni ingenzi. Porogaramu nziza zifite uburyo bworoshye kumva na porogaramu za telefoni, bigatuma kugera ku makuru y’ibinyabiziga no kuyacunga byoroha aho uri hose. Zakorewe kandi gukura hamwe n’ubucuruzi bwawe, zikakira ibinyabiziga byiyongera n’ibikenewe mu bwikorezi bihinduka. Byongeye kandi, zitanga ubufasha bukomeye ku bakiriya n’amahugurwa, kugira ngo ubyaze umusaruro ushoboka ishoramari ryawe.
