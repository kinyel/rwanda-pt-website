Ikibuga cy’indege cya Kamembe, ihuriro ry’ingenzi ry’ubwikorezi, gikeneye guhuza neza imikorere y’ibinyabiziga byacyo byo ku butaka kugira ngo habeho imikorere myiza n’umutekano. Ikoranabuhanga rya GPS rigira uruhare runini muri ibyo. Iyo ibinyabiziga bifasha ku butaka bishyizwemo ibikoresho bya GPS, abayobozi b’ikibuga cy’indege babona ako kanya aho biri n’uko bigenda.

Ibi bituma sisitemu yo kohereza imodoka iyobowe hamwe inoza uko ibinyabiziga bikoreshwa, igabanya igihe bimara bidakora kandi igatuma bisubiza ku gihe iyo indege zigeze cyangwa zihaguruka. Amakuru ya GPS yorohereza gutoranya inzira nziza, agafasha abashinzwe kohereza imodoka kuziyobora mu nzira zinoze kurushaho, bikagabanya ikoreshwa rya lisansi n’ibiciro by’imikorere. Byongeye kandi, imbibi zagenwe hakoreshejwe GPS zishobora gushyirwa ku turere tutemerewe kwinjirwamo, zigatanga integuza iyo ibinyabiziga byinjiye cyangwa bisohotse ahantu bitemerewe. Nubwo GPS ari yo shingiro ry’ikoranabuhanga, igenzura ry’amaso rikomeza kuba ingenzi. Abakozi b’ikibuga cy’indege bashobora gukurikirana ibikorwa by’ibinyabiziga ku butaka, bakemeza ko amabwiriza y’umutekano yubahirizwa kandi bagakemura ibibazo bitunguranye. Uku guhuza kohereza imodoka hifashishijwe GPS n’igenzura ry’amaso bituma imikorere y’ibinyabiziga byo ku butaka ku kibuga cy’indege cya Kamembe igenda neza nta nkomyi.
