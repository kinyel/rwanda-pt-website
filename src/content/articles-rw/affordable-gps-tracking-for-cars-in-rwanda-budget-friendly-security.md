Kurinda imodoka yawe mu Rwanda ntibisaba amafaranga menshi. Uburyo bwo gukurikirana hakoreshejwe GPS buhendutse butanga ibikenewe by’ingenzi mu mutekano ku giciro gikwiye, bigatuma kurinda ibinyabiziga bigera kuri buri wese. Ibi bisubizo bitanga ubushobozi bw’ibanze bwo gukurikirana bitabangamiye kwizerwa.

Ibikoresho bya GPS bihendutse by’imodoka mu Rwanda byibanda ku mikorere y’ingenzi nko kumenya aho imodoka iri ako kanya n’integuza z’ibanze. Bitanga amakuru nyayo y’aho iri, bikagufasha gukurikirana ingendo z’imodoka yawe no kumenya ko iri mu mutekano. Kubishyiraho byoroshye n’uburyo bwo kubikoresha bworoshye bituma bikoreshwa nta mbogamizi, ndetse n’abafite ubumenyi buke mu ikoranabuhanga.

Nubwo bihendutse, ibi bikoresho byubakiwe guhangana n’ikoreshwa rya buri munsi. Akenshi bifite bateri zimara igihe kirekire n’imiterere ikomeye, bigatuma bikomeza gukora neza. Byongeye kandi, bitanga ubufasha bwizewe ku bakiriya, bukaboneka igihe cyose bukenewe.
