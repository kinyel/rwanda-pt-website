Igikoresho cya GPS cy’imodoka mu Rwanda giha ba nyir’ibinyabiziga umutekano n’amahoro yo mu mutima. Ibi bikoresho bito bitanga amakuru y’aho imodoka iri ako kanya, bikagufasha kumenya aho imodoka yawe iri igihe icyo ari cyo cyose. Ibi ni ingenzi ku mutekano, kuko bikumira ubujura kandi bigafasha kugarura imodoka iyo yibwe.

Uretse umutekano, igikoresho cya GPS cy’imodoka gishobora no gukoreshwa mu zindi mpamvu, nko gukurikirana imyitwarire y’abashoferi no kubara ibilometero imodoka yakoze.

Ibi bishobora kugirira akamaro umuntu ku giti cye, ndetse n’ubucuruzi bucunga ibinyabiziga byinshi.

Mu guhitamo igikoresho cya GPS cy’imodoka mu Rwanda, tekereza ku bintu nk’ubusobanuro, kwizerwa n’igihe bateri imara. Igikoresho gikwiye gutanga amakuru nyayo y’aho imodoka iri kandi kigakora buri gihe nta guhagarara. Bateri imara igihe kirekire ni ingenzi, cyane cyane ku binyabiziga bidakoreshwa buri gihe.
