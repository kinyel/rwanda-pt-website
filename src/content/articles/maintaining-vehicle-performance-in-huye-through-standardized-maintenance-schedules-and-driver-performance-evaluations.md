Huye's challenging terrain places considerable strain on vehicles, necessitating a proactive approach to maintenance. Standardized maintenance schedules, based on manufacturer recommendations and local operating conditions, are essential. Regular inspections, conducted by qualified mechanics, ensure that potential issues are identified and addressed promptly. Driver performance evaluations play a crucial role in preventing unnecessary wear and tear. Training programs focusing on fuel-efficient driving techniques and proper vehicle handling can significantly extend vehicle lifespan. Detailed record-keeping, documenting maintenance activities and driver performance, provides valuable insights for optimizing maintenance schedules and improving driver skills.
