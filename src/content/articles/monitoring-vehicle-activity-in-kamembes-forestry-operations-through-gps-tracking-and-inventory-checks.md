Kamembe's valuable forestry resources require vigilant monitoring to prevent illegal logging and ensure sustainable management. GPS technology provides a powerful tool for this purpose. By equipping forestry vehicles with GPS trackers, authorities can monitor their movements in real-time, even in remote and challenging terrain. Geofencing capabilities allow for the creation of virtual boundaries around protected forest zones, triggering alerts when vehicles enter or exit unauthorized areas. GPS data can also be used to track vehicle routes and identify suspicious activity, such as frequent stops in remote locations. Complementing GPS tracking, regular inventory checks of timber and other forestry products provide a physical verification of resource extraction. These checks, conducted by forestry personnel, ensure accurate accounting of harvested materials and detect any discrepancies that may indicate illegal activity. By combining GPS tracking with physical inventory checks, Kamembe can effectively monitor vehicle activity in its forestry operations, deter illegal logging, and promote sustainable forest management.
