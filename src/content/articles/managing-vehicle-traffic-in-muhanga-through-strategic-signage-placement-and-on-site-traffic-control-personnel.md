Muhanga's growing traffic volume requires effective traffic management strategies. Strategic placement of traffic signage, clearly indicating routes, speed limits, and parking areas, guides drivers and minimizes confusion. On-site traffic control personnel, deployed during peak hours and at critical intersections, direct traffic flow and prevent congestion. Regular traffic surveys and analysis of traffic patterns inform the placement of signage and deployment of personnel. By combining strategic signage placement with on-site traffic control, Muhanga can effectively manage its vehicle traffic and ensure smooth flow of vehicles.
