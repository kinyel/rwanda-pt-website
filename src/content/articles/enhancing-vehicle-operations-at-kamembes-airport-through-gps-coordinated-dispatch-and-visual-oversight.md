Kamembe Airport, a vital transportation hub, requires precise coordination of its ground vehicle operations to ensure efficiency and safety. GPS technology plays a pivotal role in achieving this. By equipping ground support vehicles with GPS trackers, airport authorities can gain real-time visibility into their locations and movements.

This enables a centralized dispatch system to optimize vehicle deployment, minimizing idle time and ensuring timely response to aircraft arrivals and departures. GPS data facilitates efficient routing, allowing dispatchers to direct vehicles along the most efficient paths, reducing fuel consumption and operational costs. Furthermore, GPS-enabled geofencing can create virtual boundaries around restricted areas, triggering alerts when vehicles enter or exit unauthorized zones. While GPS provides the technological backbone, visual oversight remains crucial. Airport personnel can monitor vehicle activity on the ground, ensuring adherence to safety protocols and addressing any unforeseen issues. This combination of GPS-coordinated dispatch and visual oversight ensures seamless and efficient ground vehicle operations at Kamembe Airport.
